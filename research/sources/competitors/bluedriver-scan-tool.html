    What are ENHANCED diagnostics?

    All vehicles with an OBD2 port are required to output basic information when the Check Engine Light comes on. However, a lot of important information (such as ABS, Airbag, Climate Control, etc.) is left behind by the other scan tools. Only BlueDriver gives you the complete set of diagnostics on a wide range of manufacturers.

    Step One: Get Ready

    · Unpair any previously connected BlueDriver devices.

    · Turn on Bluetooth on your smartphone.

    · Download the BlueDriver App from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).

    · Launch the app.

    · Create an account or log in with your existing credentials.

    Step 2: Allow Permissions

    When prompted, allow the following app permissions:

    · Bluetooth Access – required for connecting to your sensor.

    · Location Services (Android only) – needed for stable Bluetooth pairing.

    (Optional for Android) You may also connect manually through your phone’s Bluetooth settings.

    Step 3: Connect Your BlueDriver

    · Turn your vehicle’s ignition to “Key On, Engine Off” (dash lights on, engine not running).

    · Plug your BlueDriver sensor into the OBDII port.

    · In the app, accept the Bluetooth + Location connection prompts.

    · Wait for the connection to establish:

    · Once connected, confirm the prompt in the app — you’re ready to start scanning!
