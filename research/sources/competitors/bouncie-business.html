Essential Features for Fleet Management

Vehicle Tracking

Monitor every vehicle in real-time with precise location data. See when each vehicle is parked, moving, or idle to improve visibility, safety, and operational efficiency.

Geo-Zones

Set custom geofences to monitor vehicle activity around job sites, offices, or designated areas. Receive alerts when vehicles enter or exit to enhance oversight and security.

Driving Habits

Monitor key driving behaviors such as speeding, hard braking, and rapid acceleration. Identify patterns to encourage safer driving, minimize wear, and lower fleet operating costs.

Route History

View detailed trip histories for every vehicle, including stops, routes, and time at each location. Analyze trends to optimize routes and improve overall fleet efficiency.

Vehicle Care

Manage maintenance with alerts for oil changes, tire rotations, inspections, and more. Reduce downtime and extend vehicle lifespan to keep fleets operating at peak efficiency.

Reporting

Access trip data and driving insights through the app or online portal. Export essential information to enhance reporting, improve operations, and guide better business decisions.
