Most common questions

Do I need technical skills to be able to use Carly?

Whether you are a car fan who wants to save money by preventing issues or you're a skilled technician who wants a more convenient solution that works with your smartphone, Carly enables different functions that satisfy the needs of both. It offers you unparalleled access to your vehicle's important electronic control units (ECUs) and enables you to use advanced insights and features to get more out of your car — anywhere and anytime.

Is my car compatible with Carly?

If your car was built in 2001 or later the Carly solution should work for you and for almost all car brands in the world. You need the Carly Scanner and in case of a BMW older than 2001, you need the 20-pin scanner extension, too. The app works with both Android and iOS systems. The features your car supports depend on the model, build year, hardware, and software in the car. Plug the scanner in the OBD2 port, connect to the app, run your first diagnosis, and check which features are available for your car.

 

How do I know which features will work for my car?

To check which functions are supported by your individual car, you will need to connect the scanner and the free Carly app with Basic Features. We offer you a 14-day money-back guarantee if you only purchased the Carly Scanner so you can test it together with the free basic features of the app. If it is not as expected, you can send the scanner back to us within the given period.

Can I break my car, or could my warranty expire after using Carly?

The Carly solution is safe to use and should not break your car or affect the warranty if you use it correctly and in line with our recommendations. As it applies for every other car tool.

Can I return the scanner?

If you purchase only the scanner, you can return it within 14 days after the delivery date. If you purchased the Carly Premium Packet, the scanner is not refundable or returnable, as it was included free with your purchase.

Do I have a warranty on the scanner?

Yes, you have a lifetime warranty. We will send you a replacement if your scanner doesn't work properly anymore.
