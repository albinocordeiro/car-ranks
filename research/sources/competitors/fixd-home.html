How FIXD Works

1. Plug in under steering wheel

Every car 1996 and newer has this port (OBD2) under the driver’s side dash. This is the same port mechanics use to diagnose car problems.

2. Start your engine

Download the FIXD app and start your engine. The FIXD Sensor easily connects to your smartphone via Bluetooth.

Open the app and hit “scan” to instantly decode your check engine light and monitor your car’s health in real time.
