Secure and compliant

Build confidently with Smartcar. All of our plans include enterprise-grade security, privacy, and localization.

GDPR

Smartcar is compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Smartcar is a consent-based platform that processes only the necessary data to serve our customers.

SOC 2 Type 2 & ISO Compliance

Smartcar is SOC 2 Type 2 compliant with Security and Availability Trust Services criteria. Smartcar is also ISO 27001 & ISO 27701 compliant, ensuring top-tier information security and privacy management.

Data encryption

All requests to Smartcar services must be communicated via HTTPS and all data stored by the Smartcar platform is protected with Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) 256 bit encryption.

Monitoring and updates

The Smartcar API is constantly monitored to ensure uptime of the platform and all integrations. Our real-time error reporting systems enable Smartcar engineers to swiftly detect and address potential issues, ensuring uninterrupted service.

Penetration testing

Smartcar routinely undergoes penetration tests and vulnerability scans by an independent security company to validate the integrity of our systems and services.

Network infrastructure

The Smartcar platform is hosted on industry-standard cloud infrastructure to ensure maximum performance, resilience, and deployment of our services.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a vehicle connection?

A vehicle connection is established by a vehicle owner granting your application access to their vehicle via Smartcar Connect.

What kinds of API requests count towards my usage?

Successful API requests to all Smartcar vehicle endpoints count towards your monthly usage. All API endpoints except the All Vehicles, Application Permissions, Disconnect, Subscribe, and Unsubscribe endpoints are Smartcar vehicle endpoints.
