How to use this table?

Compatibility may vary by model and trim. To determine which models are compatible, select a specific make from the drop-down menu. For more granular information regarding a specific vehicle, please use our VIN-based Compatibility API.

When will new brands be added?

We are continuously working to launch new brands. If there is a specific brand or model you would like to see added, we recommend contacting us directly and signing up your preferred vehicle for our research fleet.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current state of Smartcar's platform and brand integrations?

Visit our brand status page (brandreliability.smartcar.com) for real time updates on any outstanding outages and incidents and our platform status page (status.smartcar.com) for real time updates on any outages or incidents on the overall platform.

Is compatibility guaranteed for all brands and models listed?

The compatibility matrix provides a preliminary assessment of Smartcar functionality across various vehicle brands, based on our internal analysis and research conducted during integration efforts. While we strive for accuracy and comprehensiveness, it is important to acknowledge that the functional capabilities of individual vehicles can exhibit variability.

‍

Will Smartcar support more regions?

If you are interested in having a specific region supported, contact support@smartcar.com to learn more.

What European countries does Smartcar support?

Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom.

Are users outside these regions supported?

Technically yes, if the modem/hardware for the vehicle originates from the US, Europe, or Canada. That said, Smartcar does not officially support integrations outside these regions.
