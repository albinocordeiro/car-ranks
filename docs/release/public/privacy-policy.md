# Car Ranks Privacy Policy (MVP Draft)

Last updated: 2026-02-20

Car Ranks is an EV analytics app that processes OBD telemetry to provide read-only KPI and ranking insights.

## 1. Data We Process

For MVP, Car Ranks may process:

- Account identifiers needed for authentication and access control
- Vehicle identifiers used to scope analytics
- OBD telemetry, such as speed, battery state of charge, charging metrics, and temperature-related signals
- Diagnostic telemetry, such as MIL and active DTC data

## 2. What We Do Not Collect (MVP Scope)

- Precise GPS location is out of MVP scope
- Remote vehicle command/control data is out of MVP scope

## 3. Why We Process Data

We process telemetry to:

- Compute vehicle KPI insights
- Generate scoped ranking views
- Support app functionality and reliability

## 4. Data Sharing and Tracking

- No cross-app or cross-website advertising tracking is planned for MVP
- No IDFA-based ad targeting is planned for MVP

## 5. Data Security

Data in transit uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS channels.

## 6. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at:

https://github.com/albinocordeiro/car-ranks/issues

This draft should be reviewed and finalized before App Store submission.
